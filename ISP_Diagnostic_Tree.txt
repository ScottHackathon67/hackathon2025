🌐 INTERNET ISSUE TRIAGE
═══════════════════════
"Can you describe the internet issue you're experiencing?"

PRIMARY CATEGORIES:
A) No Internet Connection At All
B) Slow Internet Speeds
C) Intermittent/Dropping Connection  
D) WiFi Issues
E) Specific Website/Service Issues
F) Email Issues

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A) NO INTERNET CONNECTION AT ALL
════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: BASIC STATUS CHECK
→ "When did you first notice the internet wasn't working?"
→ "Are any lights showing on your modem/router?"
→ "What color are the lights?" (Record: Power, Internet/Online, WiFi)
→ "Have there been any power outages or weather issues in your area recently?"

STEP 2: EQUIPMENT IDENTIFICATION
→ "What equipment do you have? Can you describe the boxes/devices?"
→ "Do you have a separate modem and router, or one combined unit?"
→ "What's the model number?" (Usually on a sticker)
→ "Are you connected via WiFi or ethernet cable?"

STEP 3: POWER & CONNECTIONS
→ "Is the power cord firmly plugged into the modem and wall outlet?"
→ "Are all cables securely connected?"
→ "Can you trace the cable from the wall to your modem?"
→ "Is the coax/ethernet cable from the wall firmly connected?"

STEP 4: BASIC REBOOT SEQUENCE
→ "Let's try resetting your equipment. Unplug the power cord from your modem"
→ "Wait 30 seconds"
→ "Plug the modem back in and wait 2-3 minutes for it to fully boot up"
→ "What lights do you see now?"

EXPECTED RESULTS:
✅ Power Light: Solid Green/Blue
✅ Internet/Online Light: Solid Green/Blue  
✅ WiFi Light: Solid Green/Blue (if applicable)

IF LIGHTS ARE NORMAL:
→ "Try connecting a device directly to the modem with an ethernet cable"
→ "Can you access the internet now?"
   YES: WiFi issue → GO TO WIFI DIAGNOSTICS
   NO: Continue to device testing

IF LIGHTS ARE ABNORMAL:
→ Red/Orange Internet Light: "This indicates a signal issue. Let me check for outages in your area"
→ CHECK SYSTEM: Area outages, signal issues, account status
→ No lights at all: Possible power/equipment failure

STEP 5: DEVICE TESTING
→ "What device are you testing with?" (Computer, phone, tablet)
→ "Try a different device if possible"
→ "Can you try a different ethernet cable?"

STEP 6: SIGNAL TESTING
→ "I'm going to run some tests from our end"
→ "I can see your modem is [online/offline]"
→ "The signal levels show [normal/weak/no signal]"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔧 EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT: "Your modem appears to be malfunctioning. We'll send a replacement"
🛠️ TECHNICIAN DISPATCH: "There appears to be a line issue. I'm scheduling a technician"
⚡ SERVICE RESTORATION: "There's an area outage. Estimated repair time is X hours"
✅ ISSUE RESOLVED: "Great! Your internet should be working now"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

B) SLOW INTERNET SPEEDS  
═══════════════════════

STEP 1: SPEED EXPECTATIONS
→ "What internet package do you have?" (Verify in system)
→ "What speeds are you experiencing vs. what you're expecting?"
→ "Are you running a speed test? What website are you using?"
→ "What time of day does this usually happen?"

STEP 2: DEVICE & CONNECTION TYPE
→ "What device are you testing on?"
→ "Are you connected via WiFi or ethernet cable?"
→ "How far are you from your router?"
→ "How many devices are currently connected to your internet?"

STEP 3: INTERFERENCE & USAGE
→ "Is anyone streaming video, gaming, or downloading large files?"
→ "Do you have any smart home devices, security cameras, or IoT devices?"
→ "Are you using a VPN?"
→ "What browser are you using?"

STEP 4: DIRECT CONNECTION SPEED TEST
→ "Let's test with a direct ethernet connection to your modem"
→ "Disconnect the WiFi on your device first"
→ "Go to speedtest.net or fast.com"
→ "What speeds are you seeing now?"

SPEED TEST ANALYSIS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✅ Within 80% of plan speed: Normal performance
⚠️ 50-80% of plan speed: Investigate further
🔴 Below 50% of plan speed: Significant issue

STEP 5: WIFI OPTIMIZATION (if WiFi is slow)
→ "What's the WiFi network name you're connected to?"
→ "Do you see multiple network names from your router?"
→ "Try connecting to the 5GHz network (usually ends in _5G)"
→ "Move closer to the router and test again"

STEP 6: NETWORK CONGESTION
→ "I'm checking the network utilization in your area"
→ "There appears to be [normal/high] traffic on your node"
→ "Let's schedule another test during off-peak hours"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🏠 WIFI OPTIMIZATION: Router placement, channel optimization, equipment upgrade
📶 SIGNAL ENHANCEMENT: Line conditioning, signal booster, service upgrade
🔧 EQUIPMENT UPGRADE: Newer modem/router with better capabilities
🛠️ LINE INVESTIGATION: Technician to check for line issues
📈 PLAN UPGRADE: Higher speed package recommendation

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

C) INTERMITTENT/DROPPING CONNECTION
════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: PATTERN IDENTIFICATION
→ "How often does your internet disconnect?"
→ "How long do the outages typically last?"
→ "Does it happen at specific times of day?"
→ "Does it affect all devices or just specific ones?"
→ "Do you have to restart your equipment each time?"

STEP 2: SYMPTOM ANALYSIS
→ "What exactly happens when it drops out?"
   - Complete loss of internet
   - WiFi disconnects but ethernet works
   - Slow down before disconnecting
   - Browser times out but other apps work

STEP 3: EQUIPMENT BEHAVIOR
→ "What do the lights on your modem do when this happens?"
→ "Does the internet light go off or change color?"
→ "Does your WiFi network disappear from your device list?"

STEP 4: ENVIRONMENTAL FACTORS
→ "Have you noticed this correlating with:"
   - Weather (rain, wind, temperature changes)
   - High usage times (evenings, weekends)
   - When appliances turn on (microwave, washing machine)
   - After power outages or surges

STEP 5: HISTORICAL ANALYSIS
→ "Let me check your service history"
→ "I can see [X] disconnections in the past week"
→ "The pattern suggests [line issues/equipment problems/network congestion]"

STEP 6: CONNECTION STABILITY TEST
→ "I'm going to ping your modem continuously for 5 minutes"
→ "Please don't use the internet during this test"
→ RESULTS: "I see [X]% packet loss and [Y]ms average response time"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔧 LINE REPAIR: "Signal instability detected, scheduling technician"
📶 SIGNAL CONDITIONING: "Installing line filters/amplifiers"  
🏠 ENVIRONMENTAL: "Relocating equipment away from interference"
⚡ POWER ISSUES: "UPS installation recommendation"
📈 EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT: "Modem showing instability, sending replacement"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

D) WIFI ISSUES
══════════════

STEP 1: WIFI SPECIFIC DIAGNOSIS
→ "Does your wired/ethernet internet work fine?"
→ "What devices are having WiFi problems?"
→ "Can you see your WiFi network name in the available networks?"
→ "Are you able to connect but just can't access the internet?"

STEP 2: WIFI NETWORK IDENTIFICATION
→ "What WiFi networks do you see from your router?"
→ "Are you connecting to a 2.4GHz or 5GHz network?"
→ "What's the signal strength showing on your device?"

STEP 3: WIFI PASSWORD & SECURITY
→ "Are you getting a password error?"
→ "When did you last change your WiFi password?"
→ "Let me verify the current WiFi password in our system"

STEP 4: DEVICE COMPATIBILITY
→ "What type of device is having issues?" (Phone, laptop, tablet, smart TV, etc.)
→ "Is this a new device or has it worked before?"
→ "What operating system is it running?"

STEP 5: WIFI OPTIMIZATION
→ "How far are you from the router?"
→ "Are there walls or metal objects between you and the router?"
→ "Let's try forgetting and reconnecting to the WiFi network"
→ "Try connecting to the other frequency band"

STEP 6: ROUTER CONFIGURATION
→ "I'm going to check your router's WiFi settings remotely"
→ "Let me verify the WiFi is enabled and broadcasting"
→ "I'll check for any interference from neighboring networks"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📶 WIFI EXTENDER: "Your home may need additional WiFi coverage"
🔧 ROUTER REPLACEMENT: "Upgrading to a newer WiFi router"
⚙️ CONFIGURATION UPDATE: "Optimizing WiFi channels and settings"
🏠 MESH SYSTEM: "Whole-home WiFi solution recommendation"

📞 PHONE ISSUE TRIAGE
═══════════════════════
"What kind of phone issue are you experiencing?"

PRIMARY CATEGORIES:
A) No Dial Tone
B) Can't Make Outgoing Calls
C) Can't Receive Incoming Calls
D) Poor Call Quality (Static, Echo, Dropouts)
E) Voicemail Issues
F) Caller ID Issues

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A) NO DIAL TONE
═══════════════

STEP 1: BASIC VERIFICATION
→ "Is this a traditional landline or VoIP service?"
→ "What type of phone are you using?" (Cordless, corded, multiple phones)
→ "When did you last have a working dial tone?"
→ "Have you tried different phones?"

STEP 2: POWER & CONNECTIONS (VoIP)
→ "Is your internet working properly?"
→ "Are there any lights on your phone adapter/ATA?"
→ "What color are the lights?"
→ "Is the phone cable securely connected to the adapter?"

STEP 3: POWER & CONNECTIONS (Traditional)
→ "Is your phone plugged directly into the wall jack?"
→ "Can you try a different phone jack in your home?"
→ "Do you have a corded phone to test with?"

STEP 4: LINE TESTING
→ "I'm going to test your phone line from our end"
→ "I can see your line is [active/inactive/busy/fault detected]"
→ FOR VOIP: "Let me check your internet connection quality"

STEP 5: ISOLATION TESTING
→ "Please unplug all phones and equipment from all jacks"
→ "Plug just one basic corded phone into the main wall jack"
→ "Do you have dial tone now?"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔧 LINE REPAIR: "Outside line issue detected, scheduling technician"
📞 EQUIPMENT ISSUE: "Phone adapter needs replacement"
🏠 INSIDE WIRING: "Issue with internal house wiring"
⚡ POWER RESTORATION: "Service restored, please test"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

B) CAN'T MAKE OUTGOING CALLS
═════════════════════════════

STEP 1: CALL ATTEMPT ANALYSIS  
→ "What happens when you try to make a call?"
   - Dead air/silence
   - Busy signal
   - Recording/message
   - Rings but doesn't connect
   - Goes straight to voicemail

STEP 2: SPECIFIC NUMBERS
→ "Are you trying to call specific numbers or all numbers?"
→ "Can you call local numbers? Long distance? Cell phones?"
→ "Try calling your own cell phone from the landline"

STEP 3: DIALING PATTERNS
→ "How are you dialing the number?"
→ "Are you using 1+ for long distance calls?"
→ "Try dialing 411 (directory assistance)"
→ "Can you call our customer service line from that phone?"

STEP 4: ACCOUNT STATUS
→ "Let me check your account status"
→ "Are there any restrictions or blocks on your line?"
→ "Is your account in good standing?"

STEP 5: LINE QUALITY TEST
→ "I'm testing your ability to receive calls"
→ "I'll call your number now - does it ring?"
→ "Can you answer and hear me clearly?"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔒 CALL BLOCKING: "Removing long distance or outbound call restrictions"
💳 ACCOUNT ISSUE: "Account payment needed to restore full service"
🔧 SWITCH ISSUE: "Problem at the central office, escalating to engineering"
📞 LINE REPAIR: "Outbound calling circuit needs repair"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

C) POOR CALL QUALITY
═════════════════════

STEP 1: QUALITY ISSUE IDENTIFICATION
→ "Can you describe the audio problem?"
   - Static/crackling
   - Echo
   - Low volume/hard to hear
   - Cutting in and out
   - One-way audio
   - Robotic/digital sound

STEP 2: CONSISTENCY CHECK
→ "Does this happen on all calls or just some?"
→ "Does it happen when calling specific numbers?"
→ "Is it worse at certain times of day?"
→ "Do callers complain about hearing you, or do you have trouble hearing them?"

STEP 3: EQUIPMENT TESTING
→ "Are you using a cordless phone or corded phone?"
→ "If cordless, does the problem happen when you're close to the base?"
→ "Try a different phone if possible"
→ "Are you using any phone accessories (headsets, amplifiers)?"

STEP 4: ENVIRONMENTAL FACTORS
→ "Do you have the phone near any electronic devices?"
→ "Are there any new appliances or devices in your home?"
→ "Does the quality change if you move to a different room?"

STEP 5: LINE QUALITY ANALYSIS
→ "I'm going to test your line quality"
→ "Let me call you and we'll do an audio test"
→ "I'm seeing [noise/distortion/signal loss] on your line"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔧 LINE CONDITIONING: "Installing filters to improve call quality"
📞 EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT: "Phone or adapter causing audio issues"
⚡ ELECTRICAL INTERFERENCE: "Identifying and eliminating interference sources"
🛠️ LINE REPAIR: "Physical line damage affecting call quality"

📺 TV ISSUE TRIAGE
════════════════════
"What type of TV issue are you experiencing?"

PRIMARY CATEGORIES:

B) Poor Picture Quality  
C) Missing Channels
D) Audio Issues

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A) NO SIGNAL/BLACK SCREEN
═════════════════════════

STEP 1: BASIC STATUS CHECK
→ "Is your TV turning on properly?"
→ "Are you getting a 'No Signal' message or completely black screen?"
→ "What input/source is your TV set to?"
→ "Are there any lights showing on your cable box?"

STEP 2: EQUIPMENT IDENTIFICATION
→ "What cable box do you have?" (Model number)
→ "Do you have multiple TVs? Are they all affected?"
→ "Are you using our cable box or a third-party device?"

STEP 3: CONNECTION VERIFICATION
→ "Is the cable box plugged into power and turned on?"
→ "Are all cables securely connected?"
→ "Is the HDMI/component cable firmly connected to both the box and TV?"
→ "Can you trace the coax cable from the wall to the cable box?"

STEP 4: TV INPUT SETTINGS
→ "What input is your TV currently on?" (HDMI1, HDMI2, etc.)
→ "Try pressing the Input or Source button on your TV remote"
→ "Look for the input that shows your cable box"

STEP 5: CABLE BOX REBOOT
→ "Let's restart your cable box"
→ "Unplug the power cord from the cable box"
→ "Wait 30 seconds, then plug it back in"
→ "Wait 3-5 minutes for it to fully boot up"
→ "What do you see on your TV now?"

STEP 6: SIGNAL TESTING
→ "I'm checking the signal to your cable box"
→ "Your box is showing [online/offline/weak signal]"
→ "Let me send a refresh signal to your equipment"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📺 EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT: "Cable box malfunction, sending replacement"
🔧 LINE REPAIR: "Signal issue detected, scheduling technician"
⚙️ SIGNAL REFRESH: "Resetting your cable box authorization"
🛠️ INSTALLATION ISSUE: "Checking connections and setup"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

B) POOR PICTURE QUALITY
════════════════════════

STEP 1: PICTURE PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION
→ "Can you describe what you're seeing?"
   - Pixelated/blocky picture
   - Fuzzy/blurry image
   - Color problems
   - Picture freezing
   - Picture cutting out
   - Wrong aspect ratio/stretched

STEP 2: CHANNEL SPECIFICITY
→ "Does this happen on all channels or specific ones?"
→ "Are HD channels worse than standard channels?"
→ "Does it happen on local channels vs. cable channels?"

STEP 3: TIMING PATTERNS  
→ "Does this happen all the time or at specific times?"
→ "Is it worse during certain weather conditions?"
→ "Does it happen more during prime time hours?"

STEP 4: CABLE AND CONNECTION CHECK
→ "Are you using HDMI or component cables?"
→ "Are the cables damaged or bent?"
→ "Try a different HDMI port on your TV"
→ "Are there any splitters between the wall and your cable box?"

STEP 5: TV SETTINGS VERIFICATION
→ "What's your TV resolution set to?"
→ "Is your TV set to the correct aspect ratio?"
→ "Have you changed any picture settings recently?"

STEP 6: SIGNAL QUALITY ANALYSIS
→ "I'm checking your signal strength and quality"
→ "Your signal shows [strong/weak/intermittent]"
→ "There appears to be [noise/interference/signal loss]"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📶 SIGNAL CONDITIONING: "Installing amplifier or attenuator to optimize signal"
🔧 LINE MAINTENANCE: "Cleaning connections and replacing damaged cables" 
⚙️ EQUIPMENT CALIBRATION: "Adjusting cable box video settings"
🛠️ SYSTEM UPGRADE: "Upgrading to newer HD equipment"

📱 MOBILE ISSUE TRIAGE  
════════════════════════
"What mobile service issue are you experiencing?"

B) Poor Call Quality  
C) Data/Internet Not Working

F) Roaming Issues

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A) NO SIGNAL/SERVICE
════════════════════

STEP 1: BASIC STATUS CHECK
→ "What phone model do you have?"
→ "Are you showing any signal bars?"
→ "What does your phone display show?" (No Service, Emergency Only, Searching)
→ "When did you last have working service?"

STEP 2: LOCATION VERIFICATION
→ "Where are you located right now?"
→ "Is this a location where you normally have good service?"
→ "Have you tried going outside or to a different location?"
→ "Are others with the same carrier having issues in your area?"

STEP 3: DEVICE RESTART
→ "Let's restart your phone completely"
→ "Turn your phone off, wait 10 seconds, then turn it back on"
→ "Wait for it to fully boot up and search for signal"
→ "Are you seeing any signal bars now?"

STEP 4: NETWORK SETTINGS
→ "Let's try toggling airplane mode on and off"
→ "Go to Settings and turn on Airplane Mode for 10 seconds"
→ "Turn Airplane Mode back off"
→ "Is your phone picking up signal now?"

STEP 5: CARRIER SETTINGS UPDATE
→ "Let's check for carrier updates"
→ "Go to Settings > General > About"
→ "If prompted, install any carrier updates"
→ "For Android: Settings > System > System Update"

STEP 6: ACCOUNT & COVERAGE CHECK
→ "Let me verify your account status"
→ "Your account shows [active/suspended/payment issue]"
→ "I'm checking coverage maps for your area"
→ "There [is/isn't] a known tower issue in your location"

RESOLUTION PATHS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📶 TOWER MAINTENANCE: "Network maintenance in your area, estimated completion X hours"
💳 ACCOUNT RESTORATION: "Account payment needed to restore service"
📱 DEVICE TROUBLESHOOTING: "Device settings or hardware issue"
🗼 COVERAGE ISSUE: "Recommending signal booster or alternative coverage"

══════════════════════════════

   - Outside line/cable issues
   - Signal problems requiring on-site work
   - Equipment installation/replacement
   - Complex wiring issues

📦 EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT  
   - Modem/router malfunctions
   - Cable box failures
   - Phone adapter issues
   - Defective devices under warranty

⚙️ REMOTE TROUBLESHOOTING
   - Signal refreshes and resets
   - Configuration updates
   - Account provisioning changes
   - Software/firmware updates

💳 ACCOUNT ADJUSTMENTS
   - Service credits for outages
   - Plan changes and upgrades
   - Feature additions/removals
   - Billing corrections

🎓 CUSTOMER EDUCATION
   - Equipment usage training
   - WiFi optimization tips
   - Account management tools
   - Preventive maintenance

🏷️ CASE CATEGORIZATION
═══════════════════════
PRIORITY LEVELS:
→ P1 (Emergency): Safety/security, business critical
→ P2 (High): Complete service outage, multiple services down
→ P3 (Medium): Partial service issues, quality problems  
→ P4 (Low): Minor issues, account questions, how-to requests

SERVICE CATEGORIES:
→ Internet: Connectivity, speed, WiFi, email
→ Phone: Voice quality, features, voicemail
→ TV: Picture, audio, channels, DVR, on-demand
→ Mobile: Coverage, data, voice, messaging
→ Account: Billing, changes, technical support
→ Installation: New service, moves, equipment
